Case Studies

Real-world examples of how we've helped businesses scale their operations through smart tools and automation.

Case Study 1: Retool Internal CRM

Problem

The client's sales team was constantly switching between Salesforce and Stripe, causing data entry errors and slowing down the deal-closing process.

Solution

We built a custom Internal CRM portal that aggregated data from both Salesforce and Stripe into a single, cohesive interface for the sales team.

Tools Used

Retool Salesforce API Stripe API

Results

Reduced average deal processing time by 40% and completely eliminated manual data sync errors between billing and CRM systems.

Case Study 2: n8n Automation System

Problem

Operations staff spent 15 hours a week manually downloading invoices, sorting them into folders, and updating Google Sheets for the accounting team.

Solution

We designed an automated workflow that intercepts emails with invoice attachments, parses the data, saves the file to specific Google Drive folders, and logs the entry into the accounting sheet automatically.

Tools Used

n8n Gmail Google Drive Google Sheets

Results

Reduced manual operations workload by 80%, saving the company thousands of dollars annually in operational costs.

Case Study 3: Looker Studio Executive Dashboards

Problem

The executive team lacked real-time visibility into company performance, relying on a weekly static report that took hours to compile manually.

Solution

We connected their primary data warehouse (BigQuery) to Looker Studio and built interactive dashboards covering real-time revenue tracking, marketing ROI, and sales pipeline health.

Tools Used

Looker Studio BigQuery HubSpot Data

Results

Provided 24/7 real-time revenue tracking visibility for executives and eliminated 8 hours of weekly manual reporting tasks.
