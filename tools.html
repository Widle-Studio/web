Tools & Technologies

We are experts in the most powerful modern platforms for building internal tools and complex automation pipelines.

Internal Tool Platforms

Retool

Best for: Internal Admin Tools & Complex CRMs

The industry standard for building powerful internal applications fast. We use Retool to rapidly prototype and deploy secure dashboards that connect directly to your databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and GraphQL/REST APIs.

AppSmith

Best for: Open-Source Internal Apps

An excellent open-source alternative for companies that require self-hosting for security or compliance reasons while still wanting to build UI panels quickly connected to their data.

Softr

Best for: Client Portals & Directories

When you need a polished, user-facing portal or dashboard powered by Airtable or Google Sheets. Perfect for customer portals, partner directories, or simple web apps.

Automation & Workflow Platforms

n8n

Best for: Custom Automation Workflows

A powerful, source-available workflow automation tool. It allows for advanced logic, custom HTTP requests, and executing JavaScript within workflows, making it ideal for highly customized automations.

Make.com

Best for: Complex Visual Pipelines

Formerly Integromat, Make provides a highly visual interface for creating complex data processing pipelines with deep integrations into thousands of applications.

Zapier

Best for: Quick, Reliable Integrations

The most accessible and widely-supported automation platform. Perfect for setting up immediate, reliable integrations between standard SaaS products without needing extensive configuration.

Data & Analytics

Looker Studio

Best for: Business Dashboards & Reporting

Google's robust visualization tool. We use it to build dynamic, shareable reports that combine data from Analytics, Ads, BigQuery, and custom databases into actionable executive dashboards.

OpenAI & LLMs

Best for: Intelligent Workflows

We integrate OpenAI, Claude, and LangChain to add intelligent decision-making, summarization, and natural language processing capabilities to your automated workflows.
